The Boss panel's data comes from A2 Tools' plugins (taengu/A2Tools-Plugins,
https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-Plugins, GPL-3.0):

- Enhanced Boss Telegraph (Boss Callouts), plugins/boss-telegraph/bosses.js:
  the bosses, their dungeons and names in ten languages, the lines the game
  shows for their casts, skills and buffs, and the mechanics its author
  checked by hand; built by its author from the game's NpcBehaviorCase,
  NpcTalk, NpcData, Skill, SkillAbnormal, Dungeon and L10N tables. Taken
  unchanged into qml/aion2/bossdata.js and watch.json by
  tools/aion2-boss-import.py.
- Its built-in voice, plugins/boss-telegraph/voice.js: every line in
  English, made with Kokoro (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M,
  voice af_heart; the model and its voices are Apache 2.0), as Ogg Opus
  clips in voice/.

The rules for calls and sheets follow Boss Callouts' logic.js (GPL-3.0) and
the Clicktrough Boss Notes plugin (Xzero3007/A2Tools-Clicktrough-Plugins,
GPL-3.0).
