pets.json's catalogue (the pet ids the game's pet frames may carry) and the
portraits in portraits/ come from PetTracker by 0Fox2Give
(https://github.com/0Fox2Give/PetTracker, GPLv3), as the Clicktrough plugin
Pet Finder takes them (dev/make_data.py, dev/make_portraits.py).

The pet portraits are game artwork, included as PetTracker includes them.
AION 2 names, portraits and game material belong to their respective owners.
Where each pet's souls drop (names, levels, chances, chests) is the Pet Soul
Tracker's table (see NOTICE-PetSoulTracker.txt), generated from the game
client's own data.
