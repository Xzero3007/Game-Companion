Contains information from the ProtonDB data export
(https://github.com/bdefore/protondb-data), which is made available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Any rights in individual
contents of the database are licensed under the Database Contents License
(DbCL) 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/), as the export
states. The derived data/protondb.json.gz is likewise under the ODbL 1.0, its
contents under the DbCL 1.0.

data/protondb.json.gz is made by tools/protondb-extract.py: per Steam game,
the counts of working reports, the Proton builds they used and the parts of
their launch options, also by GPU maker, distribution family and CPU maker.
Values that were cut at the length limit or that name a reporter's folders
(and values of LD_PRELOAD and *_PATH, *_RUNTIME, *_FILE, *_DIR settings) are
left out; only the option's name and count are kept for them.
